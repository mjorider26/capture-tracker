# Capture Tracker Quick Start

**SPENDING TRACKED. BUSINESS GROWN.**

Use Capture Tracker at https://capture-tracker-production.mjorider.workers.dev.

## Start your account

1. Open the link and choose **Create account** if it is available.
2. Enter your name, email, a password of at least 12 characters, and the private invitation code from the owner.
3. Choose **Create account**, then use the confirmation screen’s **Sign in** action.
4. Enter your email and password. You will arrive on **Today**.

Do not share your invitation code or password. If signup is unavailable or your invitation is rejected, contact the owner privately.

## Save it on your phone

- **iPhone/iPad:** open the link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome, open the browser menu, then choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The saved icon still requires internet access and sign-in.

## Your first five minutes

1. Open **Settings** from **More** and review the report period, Weekly Review day, and document-retention target.
2. In **Money**, choose **Add transaction** and record one real business income or expense item.
3. In **Documents**, upload one trusted PDF, JPEG, or PNG receipt and link it to the transaction.
4. Return to **Today** to see the current briefing and any items needing review.
5. Open **Review** and work through any unresolved task.

## Important limits

Capture Tracker is a private-pilot financial-organizing tool. It does not file taxes, submit tax payments, process payroll, provide legal or CPA advice, or make automatic financial changes. Ask AI is read-only. Upload only trusted files: malware scanning and quarantine are not currently available.

For help, contact the owner through the agreed private support channel. Include the page and visible message; never send passwords, invitation codes, or document contents.
