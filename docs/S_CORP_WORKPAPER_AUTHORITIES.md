# S-Corp workpaper authorities and product boundary

Capture Tracker records owner/CPA-entered facts and deterministic bookkeeping workflow. It does not prepare or file Form 7203, Form 1120-S, Form W-2, or a tax return; it does not determine tax consequences, reasonable compensation, or legal compliance.

## Current primary references reviewed for V2.1

- [IRS — About Form 7203](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-7203): Form 7203 is used by S-corporation shareholders to determine potential limitation of deductible items.
- [IRS — Instructions for Form 7203](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i7203): stock and debt basis are separate; formal shareholder notes are tracked separately; guarantees/co-signatures do not by themselves create debt basis.
- [IRS — S corporation compensation and medical insurance issues](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-compensation-and-medical-insurance-issues): officers performing services must receive reasonable compensation before non-wage distributions; the page also describes greater-than-2% shareholder health-insurance wage reporting.
- [IRS — Notice 2008-1](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2008-02_IRB): describes the S-corporation-established-plan and reimbursement facts relevant to greater-than-2% shareholder health-insurance workpapers.
- [Treas. Reg. §1.62-2 summary on IRS.gov](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/nonresident-aliens-and-the-accountable-plan-rules): accountable-plan facts include business connection, timely substantiation, and returning excess amounts.

Product copy must present these as workpaper/review context, never as tax or legal advice. No automatic deadline, basis ordering election, deduction eligibility, compensation conclusion, or tax-return position is produced without owner/CPA-entered authoritative facts and professional review.
